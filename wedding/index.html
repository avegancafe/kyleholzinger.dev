Getting there

Greenpoint is the top of Brooklyn, across the river from midtown. It is easier to reach than people expect and harder to leave than people plan for.

Subway
G to Nassau Av
Greenpoint Av works too. From Manhattan, take the L to Bedford Av and walk up through the park.
Flying
LaGuardia
Closest by a wide margin. And JFK and Newark are technically… options.
Driving
Do not
Street parking in Greenpoint on a Friday night is a character test. Take a car service.
